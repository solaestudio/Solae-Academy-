01 · How it works

Build your portfolio

From blank template to a live portfolio you can send to brands.

  1. 01.

    Pick your template

    Scroll through the 20 templates below and choose the one that matches your style. Every template has the exact same pages and content. The only difference is the look. Options range from minimalist and editorial to trendy, bold, and colorful. Click through each slideshow to see every page before you decide.
  2. 02.

    Make a Canva account

    Sign up at canva.com. The free plan has everything you need. Canva Pro adds extra features if you want them, but it is not required.
  3. 03.

    Open your template

    Click the name of the template you picked. Canva will create your own private copy to edit. Your changes won't affect anyone else's.
  4. 04.

    Add your information

    Go page by page and replace the placeholder text with your own details. Before you start, have these ready:
    • The niches you want to create content for
    • Links to all your social media pages
    • How brands can contact you
  5. 05.

    Write your About Me page

    Tell brands who you are, what you create, and why they should work with you. Keep it short and specific.
  6. 06.

    Add your photos

    Replace every placeholder image with photos of you.
  7. 07.

    Film your eight videos

    Your portfolio has two video pages with four blank spots each.
    • First video page: four Canvas style videos
    • Second video page: four traditional style videos
    Use the Solae Studio Academy Content Bank for ideas and examples of each style.
  8. 08.

    Turn off autoplay

    After you upload each video, click on it and turn autoplay off. This keeps the page clean and lets brands choose what to watch.
  9. 09.

    Fill in the optional page

    If you have metrics, analytics, reviews, or past clients, add them to the optional page. If you are just starting out, skip it for now and update it as you grow.
  10. 10.

    Make it yours

    Adjust the colors, fonts, and details until the portfolio feels like you. The template is a starting point, not a rule.
  11. 11.

    Publish it as a website

    Once everything is filled in and finalized, click Publish website in Canva. You'll get a link you can send to any brand.

That's it

You now have a professional portfolio ready to start landing brand deals.
